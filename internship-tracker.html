Notes on coverage. The large firms (DWS, Fidelity, LGIM, M&G, Baillie Gifford, Invesco, Morgan Stanley IM, Wellington, Janus Henderson, Columbia Threadneedle, abrdn, and most Tier 2 names) run structured, dated internship / graduate schemes — those are the rows worth watching for an "opens" date. Many Tier 1 boutiques (Fundsmith, Lindsell Train, Veritas, Findlay Park, Silchester, Egerton, Lansdowne, Marathon, Troy, Somerset, Sloane Robinson, Pelham, Guinness) do not run formal cycles — they hire opportunistically, so they are marked Ad hoc: check their site periodically or apply speculatively rather than waiting for a window.

Persistence. When you open this as a published Artifact while signed in, your statuses and notes are saved to your Claude account and sync across your devices automatically (look for the “☁ Synced” badge). Opened as a plain HTML file instead, edits are kept in that browser's localStorage, with Export / Import to move a JSON backup between browsers. Reset returns everything to the seeded data.
